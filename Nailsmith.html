Nailsmith

Character Info
LocationCity of Tears
RoleBlacksmith
HealthN/A

Lore

The Nailsmith is a blacksmith found in the City of Tears. This npc upgrades your nail in exchange for geos and Pale Ores. Pale Ores are items obtained from either quests or shops! Can be used to upgrade your nail.

In-Game Events

!Warning clicking this is a spoiler!

After the final upgrade of the knights nail, the nailsmith gives the knight an option whether to strike him or not with his nail as his final request.

Purchasable Items

These are the upgrades of each nail.

Sharpened Nail
The Second stage of the knights nail before the default nail, The Sharpened nail. 250 Geo
Channelled Nail
The Third stage of the knights nail, The Channelled Nail. 1 Pale Ore + 800 Geo
Coiled Nail
The Fourth stage of the knights nail, The Coiled Nail. 2 Pale Ore + 2000 Geo
Pure Nail
The Final Form of the knights Nail, The pure Nail. 4 Pale Ore + 4000 Geo
