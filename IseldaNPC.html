Lore

Iselda was known to be a fighter before meeting her husband Cornifer; she then followed her husband cornifer, she put down her sword and enojoyed a quieter life in Dirthmouth. She can be found in her store located in Dirthmouth. This npc sells not bought maps by the knight from cornifer (if found). Cornifer is a merchant found in actively and recently explored locations.

In-Game Events

!Warning clicking this is a spoiler!

Iselda Opens her store only after the knights first encounter with cornifer and defeats the False Knight.
