Hollow Knight

The Hollow Knight is a action adventure 2D game that uncovers secrets behind a ruined kingdom of insects and warriors.
Explore caves, meet bug npcs, and battle other bug-warriors all designed by team cherry.

Intro

Welcome to Hollow knights wiki!

Whether you are a beginner or have decided to play further into the game but did not have time to continue,
this wiki is for you! Welcome to your first expedition in Hollow Knight. This game wiki will talk about the basic gameplay mechanics, abilities, use of charms, and tips to unlock new unexplored areas.

Hollowknight focuses both on lore, exploring a large 2d world,
fighting bosses, unlocking abilities, collecting items and upgrading abilities or items.
