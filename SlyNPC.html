Sly

Character Info
LocationDirthmouth
RoleShopkeeper / The Great Nailsage
Health1000 (Boss)

Lore

Sly is a merchant found in the Forgotten Crossroads. Rescuing this NPC in the Insidious threat of infection, this will unlock his shop in Dirthmouth.

In-Game Events

!Warning clicking this is a spoiler!

As you progress further into the game, sly, the merchant will reveal themselves as the legendary great nailsage. Defeating him will reward the user a nail art and a charm.
