! Important !

On the beginning of the game it is important to unlock and take note of each npcs.
Each NPC either can give out quests or sell important items to progress throughout the game.

Merchants

Merchants are NPCs that provides the knight (player) items or services in the cost of geo (Ingame Currency). Geo is the ingame currency of the game Hollowknight, used for ingame items and services, Obtainable by killing bug enemies and destroying Geo rocks.

Iselda
Iselda is a Merchant NPC that sells maps and map tools in Dirthmouth in exchange for geos.
Sly
Sly is a Merchant NPC that sells general Items in Dirthmouth In exchange for geos.

Upgrade Merchants

Nailsmith
Nailsmith is a merchant that upgrades your nail in exchange for both Geo and Pale Ore.
Sly
Sly is a Merchant NPC that sells general Items in Dirthmouth In exchange for geos. This NPC can be found in the City of Tears.
